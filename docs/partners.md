## About Solution Partners
Solution Partners are companies that have expressed interest in supporting and providing consultancy for our products in different regions. 

They are not affiliated with Certify The Web, and you should exercise your own judgment before engaging their services. Please inform us if a Solution Partner fails to meet your expectations so we can amend our list.

## Become a Solution Partner

If your organization would like to offer Certify The Web customers consultancy and support within your region or sector, or if you would like to encourage your own clients to adopt our products, we are happy to list motivated companies here as *Solution Partners*.

To be listed, please provide information about your organization, its size/capabilities, and the regions/sectors you typically cater to at *support at certifytheweb.com*. There is no contractual agreement required.

Listed origanization are expected to have a reasonable amount of experience in the use of our products. See our [product overview](summary.md). If you have not yet used our software please don't request to become a solution partner until you have done so. Being listed as a Solution Partner has no benefit for licensing etc, and we do not provide special pricing to any type of organisation, but you can engage with new customers to consult and design solutions etc.

Feel free to invite relevant Managed Service Providers and technology consultancies to consider being a Solution Partner in your region by sending them a link to this page.
